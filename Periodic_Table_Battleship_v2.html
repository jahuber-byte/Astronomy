Mission instructions & notation help
  • Choose 2–5 ships before a mission. Each ship has 2–5 squares. Ammunition changes with fleet size; the default four-ship mission gives 45 shots on the 54-element board.
  • Use the simplified filling order: 1s → 2s → 2p → 3s → 3p → 4s → 3d → 4p → 5s → 4d → 5p. Ignore chromium and copper exceptions.
  • Subshell capacities: s = 2, p = 6, d = 10. Fill earlier subshells before moving on. Do not enter empty subshells.
  • Full configurations only in this version; no noble-gas shortcuts or ions. Entries refer to neutral atoms.
  • You may list occupied subshells in filling order or by energy level (for example, 3d before 4s). Their electron counts must match the simplified model.
  • Invalid entries and repeated targets do not use ammunition. A valid entry fires immediately at the element it describes—even if you intended another element.
  • There are no Minesweeper neighbor numbers. Use hit locations and ship lengths to plan your next shot.
